For test mode Razorpay gives you test credentials to simulate a successful payment without real money. Here's how:

For UPI — click UPI in the popup and enter:
success@razorpay
This simulates a successful UPI payment instantly.

For Card — click Cards and enter:
Card Number : 4111 1111 1111 1111
Expiry      : Any future date (12/26)
CVV         : Any 3 digits (123)
Name        : Any name
OTP         : 1234
